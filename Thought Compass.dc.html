0Assumptions & reasoning

Your site already does the thing this app needs to do. The oxblood margin note next to your abstract is an interior voice interrupting the official text — I took that literally and made it the whole interaction model. Voices don't sit in a chat bubble; they annotate the question from the margin, in mono, tilted half a degree off the grid.

Twelve original voices in four clusters — Ledger, Marrow, Choir, Static. No borrowed skill names, no borrowed art. Structural parity with what you're referencing, but shippable as yours, which matters more for a portfolio piece than a resemblance would.

The hybrid architecture is the argument from Fig. 3, applied. Authored rules do the work: each question carries a short condition list, and a match costs nothing and never drifts. The model is only asked when nothing matches — and the answer is cached, so it's asked once ever, not once per player. That's the distinction between your clone and Neal's version, turned into a design constraint instead of a case study.

The ¶ panel is the non-CS explainer. It's not a tutorial and it's not documentation — it's a running receipt of the last thing the program did, in plain sentences, with the four or five lines of pseudocode that did it. It only ever explains what you just caused.

note —

Eight statements and twelve voices is 480 authored lines. At full grid coverage it is 11,520. The industry line is that nobody writes that. Studios do write that — on staff, line by line, and it is the part players actually remember. So the fallback here is a budget, not a verdict: authored is the default, the model is the exception, and the panel always names which one you got.

colophon —

Built with a model, about the question of building with a model. I am not going to pretend otherwise; the front page of the site already says so. The position is not that generated writing is forbidden — it is that it should be the exception you can point at, which is why the ¶ panel names the path every single line took. The lines in this grid that matter, I am writing myself.

open —

Results breakdown by voice, sharing, and onboarding are all out of scope for this pass, per your picks. The compass is the terminal screen for now.
